### 2.1 Fitbod — Rank 1

- **A — Positioning:** AI-powered resistance-training planner, "built exclusively for resistance and strength training" (vendor FAQ, via review). Audience: gym/home lifters who want the app to decide today's workout. Monetisation: subscription only; no sustainable free tier — free trial only (3 workouts or 7 days, sources differ). US price $15.99/mo or $95.99/yr (web FAQ); App Store also lists $12.99/mo / $79.99/yr legacy SKUs. HSA/FSA-eligible. 15M+ downloads, 120M+ workouts logged (vendor homepage, via review). iOS 8.39.0 (5 Nov 2026) requires iOS 18+. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **B — Onboarding/UX:** Set goal (strength, hypertrophy, circuit, general fitness, powerlifting, Olympic lifting), equipment, session length; app generates the session. Multiple equipment profiles (full gym / home / hotel / bodyweight-only) switchable per session. UX described across reviews as fast, low-friction, gym-floor-first. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **C — Core features:** Per-session generated workout with sets/reps/weights, video demos, rest timer, logging of sets/reps/weight/volume/streaks, swap exercises, adjust sets/load. Apple Watch + Wear OS. No web interface. [VERIFIED — App Store listing via reviews]
- **D — Workout intelligence:** Strongest in set for automated programming. Algorithm uses logged performance + estimated 1RM + per-muscle strength scores, applies non-linear periodisation and evidence-based weekly volume targets per muscle, and progresses weight/reps/sets automatically. **Per-muscle recovery map (0–100%)** feeds exercise selection — a muscle trained hard recently is deprioritised. Limitation, repeatedly noted: generation is workout-level, planned before the session, **not** set-by-set real-time adaptation; and constantly varying workouts are criticised as working *against* progressive overload on the same lifts (you rarely repeat a lift to beat last week's number). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **E — Health/recovery:** Recovery model is **training-history-based, not biometric**: it does *not* read HRV/sleep from wearables (explicitly noted in review). No sleep tracking, no readiness score from biometrics. Syncs workouts to Apple Health, Strava, Fitbit, Apple Watch. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **F — Nutrition:** None — strength only; no calorie/macro/food logging. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **I — Integrations/AI/social:** AI = the workout-generation algorithm (ML, vendor-described). Integrations: Apple Health, Strava, Fitbit, Apple Watch, Wear OS, Garmin. No social feed, no community, no coaching chat described. Fitbod for Business: $95.99/employee/yr (5–100 seats). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **Sources:** https://fitbod.me/blog/fitbod-vs-strive-which-strength-training-app-is-right-for-you-in-2026/ · https://fruitpunch.ai/review/fitbod · https://www.autonomous.ai/ourblog/fitbod-app-review · https://www.corahealth.app/compare/fitbod · https://gymgod.app/blog/fitbod-review

### 2.2 Hevy — Rank 2

- **A — Positioning:** "Workout tracker with a social network attached" — logging first, community as wrapper; positioning closer to Strava than to a program app. Maker Hevy Studios S.L. (Spain). Audience: lifters who already know what to lift. Free, ad-free; Pro $2.99/mo, $23.99/yr, $74.99 lifetime (US App Store, Oct–Nov 2026). Vendor's own Oct 2026 comparison claims monthly ~79% below median competitor. 18M+ athletes (vendor), 15M+ lifters / 112M+ workouts logged in 2025 (App Store listing). 4.9★ from ~101K ratings. [VERIFIED / PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **B — Onboarding/UX:** Build routines → start workout → log. Last session's numbers pre-filled inline next to each exercise; 1–2 tap set logging; auto rest timer. Praised in reviews for speed and cleanliness. Free limits: 4 saved routines, 7 custom exercises, 3 months of graph history. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **C — Core features:** Log weight/reps/duration/RPE per set; warm-up / drop / failure set tags; supersets; plate calculator; progress charts; PRs; estimated 1RM; volume; body weight + measurements + progress photos; calendar/streaks; offline workouts; Apple Watch + Wear OS apps; web app. Exercise library ~450 exercises with demo animations + instructions. 26 free splits/programs. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **D — Workout intelligence:** **Weakest link vs Fitbod/JEFIT:** no next-weight suggestion on your *own* routines on the free tier — progression only inside **Hevy Trainer (Pro)**, which builds a program from experience/goal/equipment/preferences and applies progressive overload (incl. reps-only exercises like pull-ups, and a deload option — off by default, −40% every 6 weeks when enabled). Recent additions: Injury Management setting, Program Variety setting, full Trainer program view, Gym Profiles with gym leaderboards. No published progression formula. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **E — Health/recovery:** No sleep/HRV/readiness scoring. Watch apps log workouts; Strava sync; Apple Health integration listed. Recovery is not a product surface. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **F — Nutrition:** None. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **I — Integrations/AI/social:** Social is a differentiator: feed, athlete profiles, follow/like/comment, save others' routines, leaderboards, Gym Profiles. ChatGPT plugin + Claude connector (vendor claims Claude works on a free account). Public API (OpenAPI 3.0) for Pro users only, documented as possibly changing/discontinued. CSV export; import from Strong. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **Sources:** https://fruitpunch.ai/review/hevy · https://watchranker.com/apps/hevy/ · https://www.boostcamp.app/vs/hevy · https://gymgod.app/blog/hevy-review · https://www.hevyapp.com/compare/jefit/

### 2.3 WHOOP — Rank 3

- **A — Positioning:** Screenless wearable + membership; recovery/strain/sleep coaching for athletes and high-volume trainers. No free tier, no standalone app use — membership *is* the product, hardware bundled. US annual: One $199/yr ($25/mo), Peak $239/yr ($30/mo), Life $359/yr ($40/mo); 1-month Peak trial (pre-owned 5.0). Hardware: WHOOP 5.0 (One/Peak), WHOOP MG medical-grade (Life). 14+ days battery. [VERIFIED — https://support.whoop.com/s/article/Membership-Pricing]
- **B — Onboarding/UX:** Install app, create account, wear band 24/7; scores appear after baseline period. App-only feedback (no screen on band) — a deliberate trade-off: less interruption, but all detail lives in the phone app. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **C — Core features:** Daily Recovery %, Strain 0–21, Sleep (stages, efficiency, need, Sleep Coach bedtime), Strength Trainer (AI-generated workouts added 2026), VO2 Max, Journal + Behavior Impact, Trends, Health Monitor (Peak+), Stress Monitor (Peak+), Healthspan / WHOOP Age + Pace of Aging (Peak+), Blood Pressure Insights + Heart Screener ECG / irregular-rhythm notifications (Life only). [VERIFIED — WHOOP support pricing page]
- **D — Workout intelligence:** Strength Trainer generates workouts; Strain captures cardiovascular + muscular load across the whole day, not just workouts; Strain Coach sets a daily target from Recovery. Weakness noted by owners: strength-training trends / muscle heatmaps still thin vs dedicated loggers; no set/rep/weight logging depth comparable to Hevy/Strong. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED / USER-REPORTED]
- **E — Health/recovery:** **The benchmark.** Recovery from overnight HRV, resting HR, respiratory rate, sleep performance, skin temperature, SpO2, menstrual phase where applicable. Continuous HR/HRV/skin-temp sensing. Independent sleep-lab comparison (Schyvens et al., SLEEP Advances 2025, n=62): WHOOP 4.0 κ=0.37 vs Apple Watch 0.53, Fitbit 0.42 — i.e. good but not best-in-class on staging. Calorie/step accuracy during strength training and cycling repeatedly questioned by owners vs Garmin/Apple Watch/Polar/Oura. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED / USER-REPORTED]
- **F — Nutrition:** None as a tracker; Journal logs behaviours (incl. food/alcohol/caffeine as behaviours) and Behaviour Impact correlates them with Recovery — correlation, not calorie/macro tracking. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **I — Integrations/AI/social:** WHOOP Coach / WHOOP AI: conversational coach answering from member biometrics, goals, behaviours, saved context; 2026 additions: memory, AI Strength Trainer workouts, behaviour analysis, conversational logging. No social feed of note; no HealthKit write-back emphasised in sources. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **Sources:** https://support.whoop.com/s/article/Membership-Pricing · https://www.corahealth.app/compare/whoop-alternative · https://lifestack.ai/blog/whoop-bands · https://lemonsight.com/tool/health/whoop · https://splyt.fit/wearables · https://www.ownersreview.com/fitness-trackers

### 2.4 Apple Fitness / Fitness+ — Rank 4

- **A — Positioning:** Two products conflated in the name: (1) **Fitness app + Workout app on Watch — free**, records runs/workouts, Activity rings, saves to Health; (2) **Fitness+ — paid guided-class subscription**, $9.99/mo or $79.99/yr, 1 month free (3 months with eligible device), shareable with up to 5 family members, also in Apple One Premier. Launched Dec 2020. Audience: Apple-ecosystem owners who want polished follow-along classes. [VERIFIED — https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/]
- **B — Onboarding/UX:** No programme questionnaire comparable to Fitbod; pick a workout type (12 types + Meditation), duration 5–45 min, trainer/music filter. Custom Plans (iOS 26+) auto-create prebuilt plans from favourite activities; Get Started / Stay Consistent / Push Further plans. For You recommendations from favourites. Apple Watch optional since iOS 16.1 (iPhone 8+), but live metrics need Watch / AirPods Pro 3 / Bluetooth HR monitor. [VERIFIED — Apple page]
- **C — Core features:** 12 workout types (Strength, HIIT, Yoga, Cycling, Dance, Pilates, Treadmill, Rowing, Core, Kickboxing, Meditation, Mindful Cooldown), new sessions weekly, 4K library, Time to Walk / Time to Run audio, Burn Bar (effort vs others who did same workout), Fitness+ awards, Summary tab on iPhone. [VERIFIED — Apple page]
- **D — Workout intelligence:** **The gap ASCND exploits:** classes are fixed, do not adapt to weights lifted last week, do not log sets/reps/weights, do not prescribe next load. Strength classes use dumbbells/bands/bodyweight, 10–45 min; "Progressive Strength Series" / 30-Day Strength Challenge give *programme-level* progression, not per-lift overload. No 1RM, volume, or PR tracking. Recurring user complaint: "After six months, I plateaued" / "I wish it tracked my reps." [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED / USER-REPORTED]
- **E — Health/recovery:** Deepest native integration: real-time HR/calories/rings on-screen during class, all workouts sync to Health, trends in Health app. **But:** no HRV-based readiness score in Fitness+ itself; recovery insight is whatever Apple Health shows. Context from ASCND memory: iOS 27.3 beta (Nov 2026) brings Health redesign — Insights + Longevity/Health Age, Vitals split night/day — public release expected Dec 2026; this is the platform threat, not Fitness+ alone. [VERIFIED (Apple) / PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED for iOS 27 — per ASCND brief context, not re-verified here]
- **F — Nutrition:** None. [VERIFIED — absence from Apple page]
- **I — Integrations/AI/social:** Apple-only (no Android, no non-Apple watch). Apple Music integration, Artist Spotlight, Audio Focus. Strava collaboration (2025) for social sharing of runs/rides. No AI coach, no community feed, no nutrition. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **Sources:** https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/ · https://www.igeeksblog.com/how-to-use-apple-fitness-plus/ · https://www.runandgrow.com/blog/strava-vs-apple-fitness · https://trainera.fit/blogs/trainera-vs-apple-fitness-plus · https://askvora.com/alternatives/apple-fitness-plus

### 2.5 Strong — Rank 5

- **A — Positioning:** "The simplest and most intuitive workout tracker" (App Store). Oldest in set (App Store since 2011), Singapore-based Strong Fitness PTE Ltd. Audience: beginners through powerlifters who want a fast log, not a coach. Free forever for logging (unlimited workouts, no ads); PRO $4.99/mo, $19.99/6 mo, $29.99/yr, $99.99 lifetime (US App Store; other SKUs $3.99/mo, $23.99/yr, $79.99 lifetime also listed — check at checkout). 4.9★ from ~109K ratings; 1.2M+ downloads claimed. [VERIFIED — App Store listing]
- **B — Onboarding/UX:** Minimal onboarding; start workout, add exercises, type sets. Previous-session numbers shown inline. Auto rest timer. Praised for speed; criticised for dated design and for watch-sync overwrites losing workouts (see §3). Free cap: 3 custom routines. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **C — Core features:** Set/rep/weight logging; warm-up / failure / drop set tags; supersets/grouped exercises; assisted-bodyweight + duration exercises; plate + warm-up calculators (PRO); exercise library with animated videos; body measurements + Apple Health support; Cloud Sync; CSV export; full Apple Watch app (logs with or without iPhone). [VERIFIED — App Store listing]
- **D — Workout intelligence:** **Deliberately manual:** charts for volume + 1RM progression, PRs, estimated 1RM, total weight lifted — but **no next-weight suggestion ever** (confirmed in head-to-head testing, Nov 2026: "Strong never suggests a weight"). Routine builder supports periodisation structures in principle, but progression decisions stay with the user. This is both its appeal (no black box) and its ceiling. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **E — Health/recovery:** Body measurements only; Apple Health sync for weight/vitals. No sleep/HRV/readiness. [VERIFIED — App Store listing]
- **F — Nutrition:** None. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **I — Integrations/AI/social:** No social feed, no AI, no community. Integrations: Apple Health, Apple Watch. CSV export is the portability story. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **Sources:** https://apps.apple.com/lk/app/strong-workout-tracker-gym-log/id464254577 · https://bilt.me/apps/strong · https://www.sensai.fit/blog/strong-app-review-2026 · https://livingoutlook.com/training/strong-workout-tracker-review · https://shakeyapp.com/guides/best-progressive-overload-app

### 2.6 JEFIT — Rank 6

- **A — Positioning:** Longest-running comprehensive platform (13M+ users claimed); "most complete workout platform" (vendor blog). Audience: serious lifters/bodybuilders wanting DB + plans + AI in one app. Free (ad-supported per some sources; other 2026 comparisons say free tier is ad-free but heavily promotes Elite — **conflict, NOT_VERIFIED** which is current). Elite $12.99/mo or $69.99/yr, 7-day trial; Iron Points redeemable for a free week. iOS rating 4.8, Android 4.45 (vendor blog, Jan 2026). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]

- **C — Core features:** 1,400+ exercises with HD video + animations + instructions (largest DB in set); custom workouts/exercises; supersets/circuits; custom rest timer/stopwatch/countdown; set tags (warm-up/working/drop/failure); body weight/body-fat/measurements; Apple Watch + Wear OS live sync; web app. [VERIFIED — App Store listing]
- **D — Workout intelligence:** **Elite-gated AI:** Adaptive Plan (personalised plan from goal/level/equipment, mesocycle + progressive-overload science, adapts weekly); AI progressive-overload system suggesting next weight/reps from past logs; 4 AI engines (Strength, Movement Balance, Stimulus Volume, NSPI — North Star Progress Index, a composite progress score); muscle recovery chart (which muscles are ready vs need rest); muscle map; automatic 1RM; volume/muscle-focus/weekly-sets-per-muscle charts; deload weeks planned in. Free tier gets basic logging + community routines. [VERIFIED — App Store listing; vendor blog]
- **E — Health/recovery:** Muscle recovery chart (training-load-based, not biometric HRV/sleep). No sleep tracking, no readiness score from wearables. Apple Health / Health Connect sync listed. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]

- **I — Integrations/AI/social:** Community: shared routines, challenges, leaderboards, 13M+ members. Integrations: Strava, Apple Health, Health Connect. AI is the Elite upsell. CSV export on web; no import (vs Hevy imports from Strong). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]

### 2.7 MacroFactor — Rank 7

- **A — Positioning:** Premium macro tracker + nutrition coach, from Stronger By Science (evidence-based branding). Audience: lifters / dieters who want targets that adapt, not a static calorie budget. **No free tier** — 7-day trial only, then $11.99/mo, $47.99/6 mo, $71.99/yr ($5.99/mo equiv.). Ad-free. App Store v5.8.13 (Nov 2026). [VERIFIED — App Store listing]
- **B — Onboarding/UX:** Goal (lose/maintain/gain), diet style (balanced, low-carb, keto, carb-focused), Coached / Collaborative / Manual mode, different macros on different days. Timeline-style food log (not locked to meal slots). Logging by AI photo, meal description, speech-to-text, barcode, nutrition-label scan — marketed as "fastest macro tracker". Adherence-neutral UX: no warnings/red numbers when over target (explicit vendor positioning vs rivals). [VERIFIED — App Store listing]
- **C — Core features:** Food + meal tracker (macros + micronutrients, detailed per-meal/day breakdowns), verified DB 1.36M+ entries, custom foods/recipes, copy/paste + smart history, weight trend, habit tracker, period tracker, data insights/visualisation, dark mode. **MacroFactor Workouts** (separate/newer): set logging, expert-designed adaptive programs, periodisation, custom exercises, rest timers, gym profiles — 7-day trial; Apple Watch support and PPL auto-generation were *requested, not confirmed* in user comments (Jan 2026). [VERIFIED for nutrition; PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED / USER-REPORTED for Workouts]
- **D — Workout intelligence:** Via MacroFactor Workouts only: adaptive programs adjusting to progress/strength/endurance. Depth vs Hevy/Strong **NOT_VERIFIED** — no independent teardown found in this pass; treat as PARTIAL pending verification. Nutrition app is the proven product; workouts is an expansion. [NOT_VERIFIED]
- **E — Health/recovery:** Weight Trend only (smoothed weight). No sleep/HRV/readiness. Integrations listed (Apple Health etc. per store copy — generic). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **F — Nutrition intelligence:** **Best in set.** Adaptive nutrition coach: each week compares logged intake vs weight trend → computes true TDEE (expenditure estimate from *your* data, not a generic formula) → adjusts calorie/protein/macro targets for the next week, detecting metabolic change and avoiding plateaus. Weekly check-ins. This closed-loop (intake + weight → TDEE → new targets) is the feature Reddit's r/fitness recommends MacroFactor for, and the one ASCND's meal plans most need. Weakness: estimates degrade with incomplete logging; no micronutrient depth comparable to Cronometer (users note missing micro tracking). [VERIFIED — App Store listing; USER-REPORTED for Reddit preference]
- **I — Integrations/AI/social:** AI = photo/voice logging + adaptive coach algorithm. No social feed/community described. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **Sources:** https://apps.apple.com/az/app/macrofactor-macro-tracker/id1553503471 · https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sbs.diet&hl=en_IE · https://www.mycallie.app/en/blog/best-calorie-tracker-app-reddit · https://nutrola.app/en/blog/what-does-reddit-think-about-calorie-tracking-apps-2026

### 2.8 Garmin Connect — Rank 8

- **A — Positioning:** Companion app to Garmin watches (hardware purchase required; app itself free). Audience: runners/cyclists/triathletes/outdoor athletes. Monetisation shift: **Connect+ launched 27 Mar 2025, $6.99/mo or $69.99/yr, 30-day trial** — Garmin's first subscription, controversial with a fanbase that bragged "Garmin doesn't charge". Vendor commitment: all pre-existing features/data remain free. New screenless band CIRQA announced Jul 2026 (Body Battery, HRV Status, sleep, training readiness, up to 10 days, no subscription). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **B — Onboarding/UX:** Watch-first; app is dashboard + planning + history. Repeated complaints about app UX: "terrible app, particularly the new layout", strength-training editing broken after updates, iOS sync/pairing failures (see §3). [USER-REPORTED]
- **C — Core features:** Full activity history + GPS, maps/navigation (downloadable topo on capable watches), structured + custom workouts, free Garmin Coach adaptive plans (5K through marathon, cycling, strength), LiveTrack, challenges/badges, Connect IQ store. Strength logging on watch + app (edit capability is a known pain point). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **D — Workout intelligence:** Training Status / Training Load / acute load, VO2 Max, Fitness Age, race predictions (Connect+ adds dynamic race-time updates), Garmin Coach plans, suggested workouts (Connect+ AI training advice with pace/interval structure synced to watch). No set/rep/weight progression engine comparable to Hevy/Strong; strength is a logged activity, not a programmed progression. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **E — Health/recovery:** **Free with hardware — the counter-model to WHOOP/Oura.** HRV Status (overnight HRV, 3-week baseline, 7-day average labelled Balanced/Unbalanced/Low/Poor), Body Battery (energy through the day), sleep score + coaching, Pulse Ox, stress, respiration, SpO2, menstrual/pregnancy tracking, Training Readiness + recovery time. Independent sleep-lab comparison: Garmin Vivosmart 4 κ=0.21 — weakest staging agreement in that study (older device; treat as dated, not current-model verdict). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **F — Nutrition:** None as a tracker; calorie burn estimates from activities only. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **I — Integrations/AI/social:** Syncs to Strava, MyFitnessPal, Apple Health etc. Social: challenges, connections, leaderboards. AI: Active Intelligence (Connect+). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]

### 2.9 Strava — Rank 9

- **A — Positioning:** Social training log + network for runners/cyclists (founded 2009, SF). 200M+ users in 185+ countries (press materials); ~180M+ registered per Dec 2025 press. Monetisation: free recording + social; subscription $11.99/mo or $79.99/yr (US, Oct–Nov 2026); Family $139.99/yr (up to 4); Strava + Runna bundle $149.99/yr; student $39.99/yr (50% off, annual, web only); 25% off for US teachers/medical/military. Acquired Runna (May 2025) and The Breakaway; training plans now live in Runna, not Strava. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **B — Onboarding/UX:** Record from phone / Apple Watch / Wear OS, or import from Garmin/Fitbit/COROS/Wahoo/Peloton. Feed-first UX: kudos, comments, clubs, challenges. Free tier records 50+ sport types. Friction points: upsell banners, paywall reminders (see §3). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **C — Core features:** GPS activity recording, segments + leaderboards, route builder + suggested/offline routes (subscriber), Beacon live location (free), challenges, clubs, training history, Fitness Score, effort across activities, Athlete Intelligence (generative-AI summaries of runs/rides/walks/hikes, subscriber). **Oct 2026: coach-designed strength workouts with live set/rep tracking for subscribers** (one sample workout free) — Strava entering ASCND's strength space. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **D — Workout intelligence:** Training-load / Fitness & Freshness model (subscriber) — consistently rated *less accurate* than Garmin Training Readiness / TrainingPeaks ATL/CTL / Polar Recovery Pro. Running plans outsourced to Runna ($19.99/mo or $119.99/yr standalone). Strength: new, thin, subscriber-only. No exercise DB / progressive-overload engine for lifting. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED / USER-REPORTED]
- **E — Health/recovery:** No native sleep/HRV/readiness hardware or scoring; imports data from connected devices/apps. Fitness Score is a training-load proxy, not a recovery score. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **F — Nutrition:** None. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **I — Integrations/AI/social:** **Social is the moat:** segments (crowdsourced GPS leaderboard database), clubs, kudos, challenges, feed — the network ASCND community should study. Integrations: thousands of devices/apps. AI: Athlete Intelligence summaries. API controversy: Nov 2024 developer-agreement rewrite restricted third-party apps to showing users only their own data, no analysis/AI on top; Jun 2026: paid API access for developers + login walls on previously public profiles (ahead of IPO) — trust damage with the developer ecosystem. [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]

### 2.10 MyFitnessPal — Rank 10

- **A — Positioning:** Largest calorie counter by food count; 200M registered users claimed. Audience: general weight-loss / macro trackers. Freemium with ads; Premium $19.99/mo or $79.99/yr (highest monthly price among general trackers); Premium+ $24.99/mo or $99.99/yr (meal planner, 1,500+ recipes, grocery lists/delivery). 7-day Premium trial. Acquired Cal AI (deal closed Dec 2025, announced Mar 2026). [PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED]
- **B — Onboarding/UX:** Profile → calorie budget from weight/height/activity/goal. Food diary UI. **Apr 2026: v26.16.0 replaced the Diary with a "Today" screen** — users report extra taps and loss of per-item copying between meals (USER-REPORTED, 2026). Free tier: food/exercise logging, macros shown, goals by percentage (5% increments), custom foods/meals/recipes, partner-app linking, printable report (web only). Ads on free. ## 1. Gamification / retention research (§16.2G)

### 1.1 Duolingo — summary of the existing reverse-engineering doc (NOT redone)

Source: `~/workspace/koa-mascot-research/working/DUOLINGO_REVERSE_ENGINEERING.md` (Phase I, 2026-11-21) + Duolingo's own blog/help pages re-checked 2026-11-28. Confidence: **VERIFIED** for the items below (Duolingo-owned publications / named designers).

**Mechanics (all VERIFIED from blog.duolingo.com / Duolingo help):**
- **Streak (flame):** consecutive days with ≥1 lesson; resets to zero on a miss. Loss aversion is the engine — "the longer a streak runs, the more reluctant users become to break it."
- **Streak Freeze:** buyable with gems, auto-activates on a missed day. Third-party analysis (Trophy Gamification Intelligence Report 2026, USER-REPORTED/secondary) claims freezes produce 4.5× longer average streaks by day 21 and a 21% churn reduction for at-risk users — treat the numbers as **USER-REPORTED**, the mechanic itself as **VERIFIED**.
- **XP:** earned for every activity; immediate feedback (XP shown before leaving the lesson screen). XP is the *common currency* tying streaks, leagues and achievements together.
- **Leagues:** 10 tiers Bronze→Diamond; each Monday 30 random similarly-active users are grouped for a weekly XP race; top promoted, bottom 5 demoted; weekly reset creates Sunday/Monday engagement peaks. **Key design choice: matchmaking by similar activity level, so competition feels winnable.** Users can opt out in settings.
- **Gems:** in-app currency for Streak Freezes, timer boosts, cosmetics, league repair.
- **Hearts (free tier):** mistakes cost hearts; 5 mistakes blocks progress until a practice lesson refills one — a soft difficulty/monetization lever, NOT a fitness-appropriate mechanic (see §1.7).
- **Streak Society:** milestone tiers — 7 days (membership + trial), 30 days (app icon), 100 days (extra freeze slots), 365 days (VIP badge on leaderboards/profiles).
- **Character system (the part that matters for Koa):** Duo is one fixed minimal construction (circle body + half-circle wings + pill feet) chosen *explicitly* so posing is cheap — "animation and illustration time go down drastically" (Tyler Murphy, Apple Developer interview). Three tracks: static Figma vector for poses; After Effects→Lottie for linear animation at thousand-file scale; Rive state machines for interactive characters (8 head × 8 body = 64+ idle variations from 16 pieces; 20+ mouth shapes per character for viseme lip-sync). Character sheets + reusable character library exist. Duo's mood is "almost entirely tied to your learning habits" (Greg Hartman) — tears when you skip, backflips when you practise. Duo communicates via text only, never talks.
- **Why it retains:** one trivially easy daily core action (a 3–5 min lesson) + loss aversion (streak) + winnable social competition (leagues) + a character whose mood mirrors the user's behaviour. Every notification/animation/currency exists to drive the *single* daily action. The Hook Model maps cleanly: trigger (streak flame/notification) → action (one lesson) → variable reward (XP, chest, league move) → investment (streak day, XP, rank).
- **What does NOT transfer to fitness:** hearts-as-punishment (punishing a missed workout or a wrong answer is fine in a lesson; punishing a body is not); XP farming (grinding easy lessons for league XP has a fitness analogue — logging junk volume — that must be designed against); streaks as the *only* identity (a missed rest day is medically correct behaviour and must never be framed as failure).

**ASCND cross-reference:** ASCND already has Duolingo's skeleton — streak + freeze + XP/levels + coins + weekly challenges + ranks. What it lacks vs Duolingo: (a) **winnable social competition** — ASCND's weekly challenges are solo; community challenges exist but there is no league/peer-group ladder; (b) **streak-society-style milestone status** — ASCND awards exist (29) but no escalating social status for long streaks; (c) **the single daily action** — Duolingo's loop is one lesson; ASCND's loop is fragmented across workout/meal/water/sleep/steps quests with no one "do this today" anchor (DailyPlan exists but is a decision, not a loop); (d) **character-mood coupling done right** — Duo's mood mirrors behaviour; Koa's mood engine exists but K-13/K-14 show the coupling is partly dead or contradictory.

- **Mechanics:** badges/trophies for milestones (first workout, 10/50 workouts), variety (try yoga/strength/HIIT), consistency; weekly workout streaks visible on home; structured multi-week programs ("4-Week Full Body Burn"); "Train and Collect" collection view. **No XP system, no levels, no leaderboards, no freeze mechanic.**

### 1.3 Finch — the care-loop mascot (closest analogue to Koa)

Sources: finchcare.com (official, via search); makeuseof.com, engadget.com, techanjan.com, findurnest.com reviews (PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED / USER-REPORTED). Confidence: **PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED** for mechanics; **USER-REPORTED** for retention effects.

- **Core loop (4 steps):** set goals → complete real-world tasks → bird gains energy → bird goes on an "adventure" and returns with a story + a question → bird grows (baby → adult), unlocking new worlds. **Partial progress is rewarded** — the bird adventures even if not all tasks are done.
- **Mechanics:** rainbow stones (currency for clothes/furniture); Micropets (100+ to hatch — each paired with a goal; 15 adventures to raise); streaks exist but are *non-punitive* (first restore free, later restores cost currency; Pause Mode preserves streaks); daily quests; seasonal monthly events (e.g. Raven's Mansion — complete adventures to unlock a Micropet); Journeys (goal groups with tiered rewards); friends (birds visit each other, send "good vibes"); mood check-ins; first-aid kit (coping tools for bad days).
- **Why it retains:** **care, not competition.** The user cares for the bird *by caring for themselves* — the pet's growth is a mirror of the user's real self-care. No guilt mechanics: "If you have a bad day and do nothing, your bird stays by your side, waiting." The adventure *story* is the variable reward (narrative, not points). Customisation (outfits, room decor) is the investment loop. Social is supportive (encouragement), not competitive.
- **Genuine value vs superficial:** the loop is genuine *because the rewarded action is the real-world behaviour itself* (get out of bed, drink water, walk) — the game never rewards in-app grinding. The risk is the inverse: the pet can become the point (users open the app to dress the bird, not to do the task). Finch mitigates this by making every reward gated on a real task.
- **ASCND cross-reference:** This is the strongest model for Koa's future (§4). ASCND already has the pieces (room, wardrobe, coins, quests, KoaGains growth, comeback) but they are *disconnected*: Koa's room speech, Gains rank, quests and shop do not form one care loop; Koa's growth (Gains) is a number, not a visible life stage; there is no "adventure"/story return for real effort; streak handling has guilt-adjacent edges (K-13 dead worry state, K-23 pause misreads). See O-13–O-16.

### 1.4 Peloton — streak machine + live social competition

Sources: onepeloton.com blog "Celebrate Milestone Moments" (VERIFIED, official, updated 2025-07-18); support.onepeloton.com badges page (VERIFIED); yukaichou.com Octalysis analysis (PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED). Confidence: **VERIFIED** for mechanics.

- **Mechanics:** milestone badges per discipline (1/10/25/50/75/100 classes, then every 50); daily + weekly streak badges (now calendar-day based, not rolling 72h); monthly activity challenges; trophy cabinet on profile; live leaderboards during classes with instructor shoutouts for milestones; high-fives (real-time social reinforcement); **Club Peloton (2025) loyalty tiers** Bronze→Silver→Gold→Platinum→Diamond→Legend with points (2/workout day, streak-length points, annual streaks double, modality-variety bonuses) redeemable for early access/apparel/event invites; Teams (100k+ member-created teams); hidden "quest" classes.
- **Why it retains:** the streak is the retention engine (Octalysis Core Drive 8, loss & avoidance — "users waking at 4 AM on vacation to maintain streaks"); the *live* leaderboard + instructor parasocial shoutout makes a solitary workout feel witnessed; milestone badges (Century Ride = 100 rides) became cultural markers, i.e. status that travels outside the app.
- **Genuine value vs superficial:** the leaderboard is genuine *during* a live class (real-time pacing against real people improves output); the streak is the dangerous part — it rewards *showing up* even when rest is correct, and Peloton counts third-party workouts toward streaks but NOT toward milestones (a deliberate integrity line: platform effort counts more). Club Peloton's variety bonus (10% for 2nd activity, 20% for 3rd+) is a good anti-monotony design.

The integrity line (what counts toward what) is worth copying: ASCND's streak counts *any* logged day (kcal>0 OR workout>0 OR sleep>0 OR supplement>0) — a very low bar that invites streak-preserving junk logs; Peloton's split (streak = any activity, milestone = platform effort) is a model. ### 1.5 Strava — segments solve the global-leaderboard problem

Sources: trophy.so "Strava Gamification Strategy" (2026) and "Segmented Leaderboards" (PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED, vendor analysis with platform data — treat numbers as secondary); omegapoint.systems (PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED); runnory.com (PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED). Confidence: **PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED**.

- **Mechanics:** **Segments** — any named stretch of road/trail becomes its own leaderboard; KOM/QOM (fastest on a segment, stealable); Local Legend (most efforts in 90 days — rewards *consistency*, not speed); Kudos (likes); Clubs + club leaderboards/challenges; monthly challenges (distance/climbing/commute, often sponsored); Year in Sport (Wrapped-style recap); personal-best celebrations; trophy cabinet.
- **Why it retains (the key insight):** Strava decomposes one demotivating global ranking into *thousands of small winnable ones*. A global leaderboard motivates the top 1% and demoralises everyone else (social comparison theory: people compare to similar others). A local KOM is winnable by an average athlete and carries real meaning in their network. Trophy's platform data: repeating (resetting) leaderboards show 3.4× higher competition than perpetual ones; social streaks average 5.7 days vs 4.0 without. Local Legend is the masterstroke: it gives non-elite users a title they can win through *showing up*, not being fastest.
- **Genuine value vs superficial:** segments are genuine — they convert the same route into a competitive course and make training *itself* the game. Kudos are low-cost social glue (visible accountability: "your runs exist publicly"). Sponsored monthly challenges are the superficial end (brand impressions).
- **ASCND cross-reference:** ASCND's community has challenges and a feed, but **no winnable local competition** and **no consistency-title equivalent** (no Local Legend). ASCND *could* do this better than Strava: strength has natural "segments" — a specific lift, a specific template, a gym — and a natural consistency title (most sessions on a given template/exercise in 90 days). This is the highest-leverage social-gamification idea in this report (O-09). It needs no realtime; weekly computed leaderboards suffice.
